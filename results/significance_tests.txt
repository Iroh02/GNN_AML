STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE ANALYSIS -- RiskMAGNN on HBTBD

All tests are PAIRED across seeds: each model is trained on the same
5 seeds over identical data, so seed i is a matched observation.

NOTE on Wilcoxon signed-rank: with n=5 matched pairs the minimum
attainable two-sided p-value is 2/2^5 = 0.0625. A Wilcoxon test can
therefore never reach p<0.05 here; it is reported in the JSON for
completeness but the paired t-test and the 95% CI are the informative
statistics at this sample size.
